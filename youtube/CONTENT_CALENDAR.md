# Content Calendar — First 12 Weeks

This is the launch plan. 12 weeks × 3 long videos/week = **36 long-form videos**, plus weekly Sunday lives and ~12 Shorts.

The calendar is anchored to the 9-phase Roadmap (`site/data.js`) and budgets a sustainable cadence given a full-time job. Difficulty pacing alternates so heavy weeks are followed by lighter ones.

| Day | Slot | Content type |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 7 PM IST | Long-form tutorial |
| Tue | flexible | Short (cut from previous week's long) — optional |
| Wed | 7 PM IST | Long-form tutorial |
| Thu | flexible | Short — optional |
| Fri | 7 PM IST | Long-form tutorial |
| Sat | — | Comment mining + live prep |
| Sun | 7 PM IST | Sunday live (60 min, doubt-clearing) |

**Buffer rule**: Always be 2 weeks ahead. If a buffer dips to 1 week, drop Shorts before dropping longs.

## Buffer & Safety Rules

- **Buffer 2 weeks ahead.** If buffer < 1 week, cancel that week's Shorts and Sunday live (announce ahead).
- **Capstone weeks (W11) are heavy.** Pre-build Capstone 1 in W7–W10 silently while teaching prerequisites.
- **Phase recaps are non-negotiable.** They anchor binge-watch flow.
- **The Sunday live is the canary.** If you're too tired for the live, you're publishing too much. Pull back.
- **Don't shift the MWF schedule.** Algorithm rewards consistency over volume.

## Update Cadence

Re-plan every 4 weeks based on:
- Comment cluster topics (skill 08)
- Performance metrics (`video-metadata-tracker.md`)
- Phase detection for monetization (skill 09)
- Industry events (new model drops, framework launches) — reserve 1 bonus slot per month

The first 12 weeks are the hardest. Most creators quit at week 6. Your buffer is your best friend.
